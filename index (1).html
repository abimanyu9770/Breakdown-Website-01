06 / Editing Style

Editing isn't about adding
more. It's about adding
what matters.

A disciplined aesthetic that balances energetic modern motion with clean readability.

01Velocity

Fast-Paced Cuts

Pacing tailored to the beat and emotion of the video. No random jarring speedups — only intentional momentum that holds attention.

Punch zooms • Speed ramps • Match cuts
02Typography

Clean Captions

Easy to read, perfectly timed, styled to fit the mood. Subtitles that enhance comprehension without feeling like clutter.

Highlight active word • Custom fonts • Sound-sync
03Graphics

Motion Graphics

Custom overlays, icons, tracking callouts, and clean animations that clarify complex thoughts and explain concepts faster.

After Effects compositing • Vector overlays
04Audio

Sound Design

Good audio makes an edit feel premium. Whooshes, risers, impacts, and balanced dialogue leveling keep the experience punchy.

Foley • Risers • Stereo mastering • Ducking
05Analytics

Retention Editing

Every second has a purpose. We identify potential boredom spots and inject pattern interrupts to keep the average view duration high.

Pattern interrupts • Visual hooks • Loop closing
06Look & Feel

Color Grading

From flat dull mobile or camera sensors to rich cinematic tones. Natural skin tones, filmic contrast curves, and consistent color palettes.

DaVinci color wheels • LUT matching • Rec709
